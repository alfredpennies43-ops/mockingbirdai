Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

Mockingbird runs on your Mac and keeps what it records on your Mac. We don't operate a server that receives your activity, and the app has no account or sign-in. Your activity only leaves your Mac if you choose to use AI planning, which sends your request and the activity details you select to OpenAI using your own API key. When you buy, Paddle processes your payment and we email you a licence key.

What the app records, and when

Nothing is recorded until you press Start observing, and you can pause or stop at any time. While observing, Mockingbird may record:

  • the app and window in front, with the window title and, where the app provides it, the document path or web address;
  • the type and label of the button or control you click (for example “Button: Export”);
  • if you switch them on, periodic still screenshots of your displays.

Mockingbird does not record keystrokes, the text you type, the contents of password fields or continuous screen video. Apps on your exclusion list, including common password managers by default, are not observed or screenshotted.

Where it is stored

In a data folder on your Mac (by default ~/Library/Application Support/Mockingbird). If you move that folder into a location synced by a service such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, that service may copy it under its own terms; the app warns you when it detects this. Observations and screenshots are deleted automatically after the retention period you choose (14 days by default), and you can delete them at any time.

AI planning (optional)

If you add an OpenAI API key, it is stored in your macOS Keychain. When you ask Mockingbird to plan a routine, it sends OpenAI your written request plus metadata for the observations you selected: times, app names, window titles, document paths or web addresses, and control labels. Screenshots are never sent. OpenAI processes that request under its own terms and privacy policy, and bills you directly.

This website

mockingbird.ai is a static site. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages or files requested) to deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't use advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it serves them.

Purchases and licence keys

Checkout is handled by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record, which collects your name, email, billing country and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details from Paddle so we can create your licence key, which we email to you through our email provider, Resend. The key contains your email, name and order number and is checked on your Mac without contacting us.

Update checks

Once a day the app reads a small public file (mockingbird.ai/version.json) to see if a newer version exists. It sends nothing about you or your activity beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP address), and you can switch it off in Settings → Licence.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email only to reply and keep a record of the conversation.

Children

Mockingbird is not intended for children under 16.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes to this policy here with a new date. Questions: hello@mockingbird.ai.
