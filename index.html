Listen, learn, then sing it back — with your say-so.

A real mockingbird learns the songs around it. This one learns the rhythms of your working day.

1. Watch, when you say so

Start observing from the menu bar and pause any time. Mockingbird notes which app and window are in front and which buttons you click. Never keystrokes.

2. Spot the repeats

Local rules find windows you keep returning to and short sequences you repeat — with the time of day you usually do them.

3. You approve

Turn a pattern into a starter routine, edit the actions and schedule, then approve it. Change anything and it waits for your approval again.

4. It runs on time

Routines run on schedule while Mockingbird is open in your menu bar and your Mac is awake. Every run is logged with links to what it made.

Questions

Does Mockingbird record my screen?

No video, ever. It records which app and window are in front, and which button or control you clicked. Periodic still screenshots are an optional extra you have to switch on; they stay on your Mac and are never sent to AI planning.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

macOS only lets an app read window titles and identify the control under your pointer with Accessibility access. Without it, Mockingbird can still see which app is in front, but patterns will be much less useful.

Do routines run when the app is closed?

No. Routines run while Mockingbird is open (it lives in your menu bar after you close its window) and your Mac is awake. If a run is missed, it is logged as skipped rather than run late.

Do I need an OpenAI account?

No. Watching, pattern detection and hand-built routines all work without one. AI planning uses your own key; OpenAI bills that usage to you directly.

Where is my data stored?

In ~/Library/Application Support/Mockingbird by default. You can move it, open it in Finder, set how many days to keep, or delete everything from Settings → Data.

What happens when the trial ends?

Observation and scheduled routines pause until you enter a licence key. Nothing is deleted — your observations, patterns, routines and outputs stay on your Mac.

How do I activate my licence?

After checkout you'll get an email with your key. In Mockingbird open Settings → Licence (or click Buy in the app), paste the key and press Activate. It works offline.
